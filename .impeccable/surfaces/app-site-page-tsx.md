# Surface brief: fde.traversaal.ai home (app/(site)/page.tsx)

Scope: the v1 home of fde.traversaal.ai. Visitor mode: **Persuade**. Two doors only: Learn (free FDE in Practice Level 1 first, then the Maven bootcamp, then team training) and Work with us (FDE implementation). The job board at /jobs is a supporting band (live role sample), not a pillar.

- Audience: engineers who want FDE skills; engineering leaders training a team; companies that need FDE work done.
- Actions: "Start the free course" (primary for Learn); "Talk to us" (primary for Work with us, traversaal.ai/contact).
- Proof: Traversaal's five-step engagement method; Labs case studies (anonymised, with source line); Maven rating 4.9 from 55; Hamza Farooq's background.
- Constraints: light page; not hand-drawn; no pricing for services or team training; no client names; job board count is cumulative.
- Memorable moment: the hero console, one AI system lighting up stage by stage from Demo to a live Production card, set on a solid coral disc.
- Unresolved: free course URL and module list; team-training destination; contact route confirmed as traversaal.ai/contact.

## Direction contract

Replaces the Field Manual (rejected 2026-10-01 as "looks like a document"; the founder wants bold, welcoming, client-winning).

THESIS: "Demo to production." Traversaal is the company that takes AI past the demo, and teaches engineers to do the same. Loud, confident, commercial; refuses document styling, hand-drawn marks, dark AI-gradient heroes and stat-card grids.

OWN-WORLD: White ground with hard colour fields: coral #FF4B2B and ink #0F1013, soft coral #FFE7DF and mist #F4F5F7 as quiet fields. Solid geometric discs and thin ink rings as the only ornament. Geist (600 display, tight tracking) for display and body and Geist Mono for file names, levels and data, matching the Arya landing page (user-pinned 2026-10-02). Pill buttons, 20 to 34px radii, neutral elevation shadows only. Text on coral is always ink.
